## Objetivo

WorkflowTracker had 48.63% branch coverage (75 uncovered branches, the second largest gap in the frontend); only its accessibility attributes were tested. Fourteen behaviour tests raise it to 91.78% and fail when the Escape handler, the backdrop propagation guard or the re-entry gating is broken.

## What Changes

- `workflow-tracker-behavior.test.tsx` (14): empty trace, modal open and close paths, transitions with conditions, history, unknown-node fallback, re-entry badge and timeline only on the inscription node, focus ring, reduced motion, unknown status, invalid transitions and fork layering.
