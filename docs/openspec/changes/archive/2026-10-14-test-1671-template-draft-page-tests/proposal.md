## Objetivo

After #1670 the budget templates page (49% lines) and the registration drafts page (35%) are two of the largest gaps and carry rules only Playwright protects. Behaviour tests for both raised measured coverage from 71.48/64.03/67.38/72.56 to 72.81/64.90/68.77/73.99, and failed when the PUT path, the key lock, the status gating or the field gating was broken.

## What Changes

- `templates-admin-page.test.tsx` (6 tests): list, selection-required guard, edit through the composite key with locked key fields, delete through the key, delete failure toast, cost button gated on a procedure type.
- `registration-drafts-page.test.tsx` (9 tests): no load before a search, the step each status offers, submit/observe/register gated on their required fields with the PUT body of each, rejected step keeps the dialog open, report download failure.
- Vitest floors rise from 69/62/65/70 to 70/63/66/71 with the guard test, `code-quality.md` and the testing inventory in step.
