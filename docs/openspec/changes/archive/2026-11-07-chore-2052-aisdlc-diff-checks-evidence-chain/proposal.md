## Objetivo

Notaire's Foreman merge rule, its plan and its evidence are prose or plain files that nothing checks: the Owner-only clause has no command, a proposal does not bound the diff (audit S2), and the local worker was observed editing its ledger. agentic-os, gentle-ai and factory-control each solved one of these; the stack-agnostic kit should carry the mechanism so any adopter gets it.

## What Changes

- `aisdlc protected`: fails when the diff touches a `[protected]` path.
- `aisdlc size`: fails over a review budget (`[size] limit`, default 400), excluding globs and binaries.
- `aisdlc scope`: fails on a changed path outside the change record's `### Write set`; NOT EVALUABLE (or failure with `--strict`) when no record declares one; HTML comments ignored.
- Ledger and cost log hash-chained; `aisdlc evidence verify [--anchor D]` detects edits, deletions, reordering and, with an anchor, a truncated tail; an empty ledger is NO EVIDENCE.
- `doctor` warns when an agent role may merge and no owner-only paths exist.
- Kit policy template: decision packet, deviations, bounded corrections, risk line, incident lowers autonomy.
