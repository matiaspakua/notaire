## Objetivo

IdentificationTypeController injects two repositories, imports the JPA entity and holds the business rules (unique name ignoring case, in use by people), so the rules cannot be tested without Spring and a database. It is the pilot of the strict form of ADR-030 before the other catalogs copy it.

## What Changes

- A pure domain IdentificationType with factories, and domain exceptions that carry no HTTP status.
- Inbound ports (query, manage), outbound ports (catalog, usage) and two use cases holding the rules.
- A persistence adapter mapping the legacy entity, which stays until Person migrates.
- The controller calls the inbound ports only; a controller-scoped advice maps the domain exceptions to the same 404 and 409 responses as before.
- The controller's baseline entries are removed.
