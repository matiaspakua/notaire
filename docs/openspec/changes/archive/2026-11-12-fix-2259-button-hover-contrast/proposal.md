## Objetivo

A batch rendered pass over deeds, budgets, copies, items, people and the earlier modules (Chromium, 1280 px and 320 px, axe) found axe color-contrast (serious) on the primary button while hovered: `hover:bg-primary/90` lightens the brand blue towards white, which drops white text from 4.7:1 at rest to 4.01:1 on hover (computed and measured: rgb(255,255,255) on oklab(0.563 -0.046 -0.187 / 0.9)). It affects every default and apple button; the destructive variant stays above 4.5:1.

## What Changes

- `button.tsx`: the default and apple variants hover to the existing darker `primary-text` token (hsl 210 100% 38.4%) instead of primary at 90% opacity, so hover is darker than rest and above 4.5:1.
- New unit test computes each filled variant's hover background blended on white and requires 4.5:1 against its foreground (default and apple failed at 4.01 first).
- Evidence: deeds, budgets, copies, items, people, payments and managements pass axe, have no sideways scroll at 320 px and tie every required error to an invalid control.
