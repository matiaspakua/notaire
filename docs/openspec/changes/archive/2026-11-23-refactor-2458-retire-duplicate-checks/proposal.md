## Objetivo

Second slice of epic #2456. `check-commit-messages.sh`, `check-tdd-evidence.sh` and `agent-context-budget.py` duplicate `aisdlc commits`, `aisdlc tdd` and `aisdlc budget`. Two copies drift (the kit rejects Notaire's `design` type; the kit cannot check test-before-code order or honour the exception label), and Notaire does not prove the kit runs its own process.

## What Changes

Add to the kit what Notaire's scripts check that the kit does not: `[tdd] order` (test-before-code, naming the first bad commit) and `[tdd] waive_labels` with `aisdlc tdd --labels`. Move Notaire's commit types, test paths, order and waiver into `.aisdlc/aisdlc.toml`. Switch preflight, `sdlc-process.yml` and the profile gate to `aisdlc`; delete the three scripts and port their tests.
