## Objetivo

Putting a procedure folder on hold still rejected a blank reason with `toast.error` only. The create dialog was already fixed. This is the remaining toast-only client check on the managements page.

## What Changes

- The hold-reason field uses `FormField error` when confirm is pressed with a blank reason.
- Typing a non-blank reason, or closing the dialog, clears that error.
- No toast is shown for the blank reason, and no request is sent.

## Reglas de negocio

| Rule | Source | New / Changed / Made explicit |
|------|--------|-------------------------------|
| A required-field error is a FormField error tied to its control, not a toast alone | FORMS-AUDIT-PLAN checklist (Errors); #2088 convention | New |
