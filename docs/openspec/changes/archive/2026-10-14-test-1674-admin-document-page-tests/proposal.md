## Objetivo

After #1673 the measured coverage was 76.00/67.29/70.92/77.37, 2.6 points of lines short of the 80% target. Behaviour tests for the document type catalog, the budget concept catalog, the role and user administration, the external entity documents page and the required documents page raised it to 78.72/70.11/74.16/80.02 and failed when the due-days rule, the expiry clear, the conflict message, the in-use guards, the module toggle, the role re-assignment, the movement mapping or the print and empty-state gating was broken.

## What Changes

- `document-types-admin-page.test.tsx` (7), `concepts-admin-page.test.tsx` (7), `roles-admin-page.test.tsx` (6), `users-admin-page.test.tsx` (6), `external-entity-documents-page.test.tsx` (6) and `required-documents-page.test.tsx` (4): the rules named in the issue, mocking the api-client helpers and driving the Radix select with the usual jsdom pointer polyfills.
- Vitest floors rise from 74/65/68/75 to 76/68/72/78 with the guard test, `code-quality.md` and the testing inventory in step.
