## Objetivo

Seven Playwright scenarios are unconditional test.skip placeholders citing missing UI. Four of those claims are stale: the deeds, document types and concepts pages have search inputs and the budgets page has a template action, so CU39, CU62, CU65 and CU66 have no executed UI workflow although they could.

## What Changes

- CU62, CU65, CU66 search scenarios and the CU39 template scenario become real passing tests.
- The feature-gap inventory guard and the E2E mapping document are updated from 7 to 3 documented gaps.
- The remaining gaps (CU15 payment details, CU48 notary page and form) keep their documented reason and issue citation.
