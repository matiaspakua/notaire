## Why

## Objetivo

The foundation slice imported no OpenTelemetry SDK and did not test TRACEPARENT or OTEL_SDK_DISABLED. Factory lead-time and cost need a safe telemetry seam without a hosted collector or GenAI convention claims.

## What Changes

Add TelemetryPort (stdlib no-op by default; real OTel NoOpTracerProvider when enabled with exporter=none), redact prompts/diffs/secrets, wire stage/cost lead-time hooks, and a minimal OpenFeature-shaped FeatureFlagPort stub that cannot relax core/security invariants.
