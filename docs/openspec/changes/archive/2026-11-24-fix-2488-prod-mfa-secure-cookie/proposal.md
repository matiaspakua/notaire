# Production deployments wire the MFA key, the MFA policy and the secure cookie

| Gate 1 status | passed |

## Objetivo

MFA shipped under #1640, but only the development compose passed its settings. In production the TOTP cipher had no key (enrolment failed, ENFORCE impossible) and the JWT cookie went out without Secure.

## What Changes

Add the three variables to the backend of `docker-compose.prod.yml` and of the Kustomize base (key from `notaire-secrets`, placeholder in `secrets.yaml`); document them in `.env.example` and the deployment guide; pin them in infra tests.
