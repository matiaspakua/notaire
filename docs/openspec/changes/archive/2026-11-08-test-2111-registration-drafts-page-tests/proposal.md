## Objetivo

The registration drafts page had 61% branch coverage (20 uncovered branches). Twenty-two behaviour tests raise it to 90% and fail when the deed requirement, the step conditions, the dialog closing on success or the server's reason is broken.

## What Changes

`registration-drafts-page-actions.test.tsx` (22): generating a draft for a chosen deed with success, the server's reason and the fallback, the status labels, the loading text, the submit, observe and register steps closing on success and keeping their dialog on failure, cancelling, and the report download lock. The deed picker is replaced by a small test double.
