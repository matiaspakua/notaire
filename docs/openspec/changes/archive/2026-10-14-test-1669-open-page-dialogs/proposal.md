## Objetivo

Rendering the pages with a populated list left the dialog, form and confirmation branches behind each button unexecuted. Clicking every enabled button once raised measured coverage from 64.27/57.62/59.06/65.26 to 69.67/62.75/65.79/70.68.

## What Changes

- `dashboard-pages-render.test.tsx` clicks every enabled button of each populated page once and asserts nothing throws; the fixture gains the composite key the templates page reads.
- Vitest floors rise from 62/55/57/63 to 67/60/63/68 (statements/branches/functions/lines) with the guard test and `code-quality.md` in step.
