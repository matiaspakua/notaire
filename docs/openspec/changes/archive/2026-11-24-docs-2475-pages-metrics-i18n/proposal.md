## Objetivo

The Owner asked for current numbers on every Pages page, correct Spanish and a coherent style. The audit found 240 English lines on Spanish pages, untranslated accessibility labels, a broken diagram, typed numbers that contradicted their sources (16 stations, a 70/25 coverage floor, an 80% target, four AI tools) and a roadmap snapshot five weeks old.

## What Changes

Regenerate all snapshots; derive floors, target and AI tools in metrics.json; one DOM localisation for all pages (attributes and late content); about 830 Spanish pairs; a Spanish coverage smoke test; guards for factory-line counts and Mermaid semicolons; a read-only roadmap snapshot mode.
